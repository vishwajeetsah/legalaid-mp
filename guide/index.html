Know your rights

Free legal aid is a right, not a favour. Here is, in simple words, who can get it, what it includes, what happens after you apply, and how to get help even before an arrest.

Who can get free legal aid

Under Section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987, the following persons are entitled to free legal aid whatever their income:

  • A womanSection 12(c)
  • A child (under 18 years)Section 12(c)
  • Member of SC / ST communitySection 12(a)
  • Industrial workmanSection 12(f)
  • Currently in custodySection 12(g)
  • Victim of human trafficking or begar (forced labour)Section 12(b)
  • A person with disability or mental illnessSection 12(d)
  • Victim of a disaster, caste atrocity or violenceSection 12(e)
  • Any other person whose annual family income is below the limit prescribed by their State Government (Section 12(h))Section 12(h)
