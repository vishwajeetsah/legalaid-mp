You do not qualify for free legal aid

Why

  • You do not fall under a protected category, and your annual family income is above the limit prescribed by your State Government under Section 12(h) of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987.

You can still get help

  • Pre-Litigation Mediation at your DLSA

    Settle the matter without going to court, at low or no cost.

    Find my legal aid office

  • National Lok Adalat

    Held periodically for quick settlement of pending and pre-litigation matters.

  • NALSA Toll-Free Helpline: 15100

    Free legal guidance over the phone.
